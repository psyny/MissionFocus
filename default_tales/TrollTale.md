# The Tale of the Trolls: design notes

How the built-in tale `TrollTale.lua` was put together, and why. Read this before changing the route.

The tale is generated by `troll_tale.py` in the private `MissionFocusTools` repository (cloned next to `MissionFocus`); change the generator, not `TrollTale.lua`, then run `Tools/check_tales.py`. See `MissionFocusTools/README.md`.

## Goal

Tell the story of the trolls, the Darkspear first and the troll civilizations around them (Gurubashi, Drakkari, Zandalari, Amani), through the quests that tell it. It is played as a **Darkspear troll of the Horde**, so the tale is Horde-only (`"faction": "horde"`). It is made for **Retail** (`"game": "retail"`): a Forever version would need its own route.

It is a lore route, not a speed-leveling guide. When lore and mechanics disagreed, **lore won**, unless the game would block the player (a quest that cannot be picked up, a zone that does not scale).

## Shape: leveling first, history after

Troll history is not told in order of expansions, and a new character cannot play it in order anyway: old content only scales through Chromie Time, and some of it (dungeon story, raids) is only reachable at high level.

So the tale has four stories:

| Story | What | Why there |
|---|---|---|
| **Rise of the Darkspear** | Echo Isles, Stranglethorn, Zandalar | The troll content that can level a character. Played first, as the leveling path. |
| **Echoes of the Old Empires** | Zul'Drak, Rise of the Zandalari, Isle of Thunder, Legion's opening | History that happened earlier but is best played after leveling, at level 80, as legacy content. |
| **The Shadow Hunter's Last Journey** | BfA war campaign and raids, Vol'jin's spirit, Night Fae, Darkspear heritage | Vol'jin's arc, from his death to the end of his spirit's journey. Needs Zandalar done and high level. |
| **The Amani Reborn** | Midnight | The current expansion, with the Amani at its center. Played last, as the present day of the story. |

Stories follow each other (`previous`). The order is the lore order of the arcs, not the fastest mechanical order: Midnight could be started at level 80 without stories 2 and 3, but the tale asks for them first because Midnight's Amani story reads better after the rest of troll history.

## Chromie Time

Chromie Time is what makes old zones scale to the player. It decides whether old content gives experience and is playable at the right difficulty.

- **Story 1 uses it.** Echo Isles: none (levels 1 to 10, Chromie Time opens at 10). Stranglethorn: the Cataclysm timeline (the remade old world). Zandalar: the Battle for Azeroth timeline.
- **Stories 2 and 3 turn it off.** They are played at level 80 or more for the story, not for experience. With Chromie Time off, old zones stay at their original level, so a high-level character goes through them quickly.
- **Story 4 does not use it.** Midnight is the current expansion.

Chromie Time is written in **notes** at the start of chapters and in the story descriptions, never as a hard requirement (`requires.chromieTime`). Reasons:

1. The timelines change with every expansion: which ones exist, their names, their level ranges. A requirement would block the player when Blizzard reshuffles them; a note just needs rewording.
2. The game's Chromie Time IDs are not stable, documented values.

When a new expansion changes Chromie Time, reread the notes in the chapters of story 1 and the descriptions of stories 2 to 4.

## Legacy content: dungeons and raids

Older dungeons and raids have no story mode. To see their story the player solos them at high level, which works best with Chromie Time off and the instance set to **Legacy loot**. Stories 2 and 3 say this in their notes (the shared `LEGACY` text in the generator).

Dungeons and raids are **Dungeon steps**:

- They are **main** (they count) when the draft treated them as essential: Zul'Aman and Zul'Gurub, Throne of Thunder, Siege of Orgrimmar, Uldir, Battle of Dazar'alor, De Other Side, Altar of Fangs, The Venomous Abyss.
- They are **optional** when they only add to a chapter: Gundrak.
- They are **marked done by hand** (Shift-click), because most have no quest that reliably says "done". Zul'Gurub is the exception: it is done when *Break the Godbreaker* (29241) is done. The Zul'Aman dungeon quest ID found on the wiki (11196) belongs to the original Burning Crusade instance, so it was not used.

## Levels and fillers

Requirements (`requires.level`) are used only where the game itself gates the content:

- Stranglethorn and Zandalar: level 10 (Chromie Time).
- Stories 2, 3 and 4: level 80.
- Darkspear heritage: level 50.
- Midnight's max-level campaign and the Curse of Ula'tek: level 90.

Content needed only to level or to unlock what follows is kept, but marked:

- **The Long Road** (end of story 1) is an **optional** chapter with a single note: reach level 80 however the player likes. The War Within's campaign is suggested, not required, because it is not troll lore.
- Mandatory unlocks are **side** steps: they count toward finishing (the game requires them) but sit beside the main line. Examples: the Kul Tiras war campaign before the Battle of Dazar'alor, the Night Fae chapters before *Da Boss* and *Deal for a Loa*, the Harandar and Arator chapters before the Voidstorm.
- **Optional** marks what adds lore but is not needed: breadcrumbs, side quests inside chains, the Zul'Aman side stories, *Lorewalking: Loa*.

## Steps: questlines first

Where the game has a questline for a chapter of the story, the tale uses one **Questline step** for it instead of listing every quest:

- the game tracks it (and MissionFocus asks the game whether it is complete);
- chapters stay readable (one box per questline);
- clicking it opens the full questline with its branches.

Quest steps are used where no questline exists (Rise of the Zandalari, the Isle of Thunder opening, the Cape of Stranglethorn troll chain, the Zul'Drak loa chains, the BfA introduction, Legion's opening), or where a single quest matters (*Zandalar Forever!*, *The Battle of the Bridge*, *The King's Death*).

Step titles use the chapter names players know (BtWQuests' and the achievements' names, e.g. *Portents and Prophecies*), not the game's internal questline names (*Of Prophecies and Prophets*).

## Order and parallel paths

The order inside chapters comes from the data, not from the draft:

- **BtWQuests' chain prerequisites** say which chapters must come before which. Where two can be played in any order, the tale shows them side by side (`Par` in the generator). Examples: Port of Zandalar, Portents and Prophecies and The Zanchuli Council; Harandar and Arator's journey.
- **BtWQuests' chain branches** give the order of quests inside chains, side quests included (`CHAIN` in the generator).
- Optional steps never hold the main line: the step after an optional one also waits for the main step before it.

Where the data allows parallel play but the story reads better in order (Zuldazar, then Nazmir, then Vol'dun), the tale keeps the story order.

## Where the data changed the draft

The first draft of the route was checked against the game's data. The data won:

- **Nazmir** needs *The Spirits of Zo'bal* before *A Pact with Death*.
- **A Bargain of Blood** needs Zuldazar and Nazmir; **Zandalar Forever!** also needs Vol'dun.
- **The Shadow Hunter** needs the Battle of Dazar'alor war campaign, which needs the 8.0 war campaign: both are in the tale as required filler.
- **Midnight's max-level campaign** is Foothold, The Voidspire, Gathering of the Elves, The Battle of the Bridge, March on Quel'Danas, Dawn of a New Well. There is no "The Darkwell" chapter.
- **The Curse of Ula'tek** is Legacy of the Amani, An Island of Fangs, Ghosts of the Past, Original Sin, The Battle for Atal'Utek, then The Call of the Void. *History Lesson* is part of Legacy of the Amani.
- **Night Fae**: For Queen and Grove!, Daughter of the Night Warrior, *Da Boss*, Night Warrior's Curse, Drust to Drust, The Horned Hunter, *Deal for a Loa*. All need Renown.

## Content that is gone or changed

- **The Darkspear Rebellion** (patch 5.3) is no longer fully playable: it is a **note** between Throne of Thunder and Siege of Orgrimmar, telling the player to read up on it.
- Anything else Blizzard removes later should become a step marked **No longer in the game** (`unavailable`): it stays in the tale, but is never waited for.

## Sources

- **BtWQuests** (ReferenceAddons): chains, quest order, branches, prerequisites, faction variants. The main source.
- **wago.tools** questline tables (`QuestLine`, `QuestLineXQuest`): the game's questline IDs, matched to BtWQuests chains by their quests.
- **Warcraft Wiki**: quests no local data covers (Rise of the Zandalari, the Isle of Thunder opening, *Break the Godbreaker*). They are listed in the generator's `WIKI_QUESTS` and in `Tools/known_quests.json`.

## To check in game

- Rise of the Zandalari and Isle of Thunder quest IDs (from the wiki).
- The Echo Isles questline lists every class's version of its quests; if the game's own "questline complete" answer is not available, its step may need a Shift-click.
- Chromie Time notes, after every expansion launch.

## Updating

1. Change the route in `MissionFocusTools/troll_tale.py`.
2. Raise `version` in the tale: players who added it are offered the update; their marks stay, because step IDs come from content (`q<questID>`, `ql<questLineID>`, `dg-<dungeon>`).
3. Run `python -I -X utf8 ../MissionFocusTools/troll_tale.py`, then `python -I -X utf8 Tools/check_tales.py` (it should end with `OK`).
4. Update this file with any new decision.
